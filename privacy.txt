Effective Date: August 1, 2026

Thank you for using KeyVibes (“App”, “we”, “our”, or “us”). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the App.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Piano Reader is designed with privacy in mind.

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share your personal information. Specifically, the App does not collect:

* Your name, email address, or other contact information
* Your location
* Photos, recordings, screenshots, or on-screen activity
* Payment information
* Contacts or calendars
* Device identifiers (such as IDFA)
* Analytics or usage data
* Any other information that can identify you

All score files, settings, and other app data remain stored locally on your device.

2. App Permissions

Piano Reader does not require any special system permissions to function.

If a future version of the App requires access to device features or data (such as Photos or Files), you will be asked for your permission before access is granted.

3. Data Storage

All data created or used by Piano Reader is stored locally on your device.

We do not upload your files or settings to our servers, and we do not provide cloud storage or synchronization services.

4. Third-Party Services

Piano Reader does not use third-party services to collect, process, or transmit your personal information.

The App does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking technologies.

5. Children’s Privacy

Piano Reader is not directed to children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App does not collect personal information from any users, no personal information from children is collected or stored.

6. Security

Because we do not collect or transmit your personal information, we do not maintain servers containing your personal data.

Any data used by the App remains on your device and is protected by Apple’s built-in security features and system-level protections.

7. Your Rights

Since we do not collect, store, or process your personal information, there is no personal data for us to access, modify, export, or delete.

If you choose to delete the App, any locally stored app data may also be removed from your device in accordance with your device’s operating system.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

If we make significant changes, we will update the Effective Date and Last Updated date at the top of this document. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about our privacy practices.
